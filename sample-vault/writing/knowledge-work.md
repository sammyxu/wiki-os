# Knowledge Work

Knowledge work is the discipline of turning information into insight — reading, note-making, and deliberate thinking treated as a craft rather than an accident. Peter Drucker coined the term in 1959's *Landmarks of Tomorrow*, arguing that the productivity of thinkers, not manual labor, would define the modern economy.

The notes in this cluster map the tools and traditions knowledge workers have refined over centuries, from Renaissance notebooks to networked wikis. Douglas Engelbart's 1962 vision of augmenting human intellect, later realized through [[computing/hypertext]], gave these old paper practices a digital home where links do the heavy lifting.
