# Space Race

The Space Race was the Cold War competition between the United States and the Soviet Union for supremacy in spaceflight, running roughly from 1955 until the joint Apollo–Soyuz mission of 1975. It turned rocketry from military engineering into a public contest of national prestige.

The Soviets claimed the early firsts: [[space/sputnik|Sputnik]] in 1957, the Luna probes to the Moon, and Yuri Gagarin's single orbit aboard Vostok 1 on 12 April 1961. Weeks after Gagarin's flight, President John F. Kennedy committed the United States to a crewed lunar landing before the decade's end, transforming the rivalry into the [[space/apollo-program|Apollo Program]].

## Milestones

- 1957 — Sputnik 1 becomes the first artificial satellite.
- 1961 — Gagarin orbits Earth; Alan Shepard's Mercury flight follows, its trajectory verified by mathematician [[people/katherine-johnson|Katherine Johnson]].
- 1965 — Alexei Leonov performs the first spacewalk.
- 1969 — [[space/apollo-11|Apollo 11]] lands two astronauts on the Moon.
- 1975 — An Apollo–Soyuz handshake in orbit closes the race.
